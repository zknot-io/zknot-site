Return & Refund Policy

Last updated 2026-08-05

Thirty days, no interrogation. There are a few things we genuinely cannot take back, and we would rather name them plainly here than surprise you at the point of asking.

1. The basic policy

You may return most hardware within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price, provided it comes back in resaleable condition — undamaged, complete, and with its packaging. You pay return postage; we refund the item.

We do not ask whether you used it. A sealed passive article shows no wear, so "unused" is not a condition we could check even if we wanted to — and a test we cannot apply is not a test, it is an argument waiting to happen. If it comes back in resaleable condition, that is enough.

If the item is faulty, wrong, or damaged in transit, we pay everything — return postage included — and you choose a replacement or a full refund including original shipping.

2. What cannot be returned, and why

Each of these has a real reason, not a policy reflex.

  • Applied tamper-evident seals. A seal that has been applied is consumed — removing it destroys it, which is precisely its function. Unopened, unused packs can be returned normally.
  • Custom, co-branded, and made-to-order runs. If your company name is cast under the resin, or serials were printed to match your case or batch, the article cannot be resold to anyone else. These are non-returnable unless faulty.
  • Articles deliberately opened, cut, or removed from a mounting. For a tamper-evident product that is the article doing its job, not failing. See our Terms, section 6.

Provisioned devices are a special case, and we are not selling them direct yet. A device that carries a cryptographic identity cannot be resold once it has been in someone else's custody — erasing it to make it safe to resell permanently destroys the article. When we do sell them, they will carry their own RMA procedure saying exactly that. We would rather build that honestly than fold it into a paragraph here.

3. How to start a return

  1. Email ops@zknot.io with your order number and what you would like to do.
  2. We reply with a return address and, where relevant, a prepaid label.
  3. Send it back. Keep your tracking — until it reaches us, that is your proof of postage.

You do not need to give a reason. If something about the product did not match what our pages led you to expect, we would like to hear it — that is a defect on our side and we fix those.

4. Refunds

We issue refunds to the original payment method within 14 days of receiving the return, usually much sooner. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. We will email you when the refund is sent.

If we cancel an order ourselves — because an item is out of stock, mispriced, or we cannot fulfil it — you are refunded in full, including shipping, without needing to ask.

5. HashStamp and digital purchases

HashStamp is a separate service with its own terms and refund policy. See hashstamp.io/refund.

6. Your statutory rights

Nothing here limits any consumer right that cannot be waived under applicable law. If your local law gives you a longer return window or stronger remedy than this page, that law wins.
