Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-08-05

We collect very little, we publish some things permanently by design, and we sell nothing about you. This page says which is which.

1. Who is responsible

ZKNOT, INC., PO Box 993, Salt Lake City, UT 84110, USA — ops@zknot.io. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

2. What this website collects

We do not add advertising trackers, analytics beacons, or third-party marketing scripts to this site. There is no cookie banner here because we do not set cookies to track you. If that ever changes, this page changes first.

Our sites are served through Cloudflare, which acts as our CDN and security layer. In doing so Cloudflare processes standard request data — IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp — to route traffic, block abuse, and defend against attacks. That is a necessary part of serving a website securely.

3. What we collect when you contact or buy

  • Email. If you email us, we hold your message and address so we can reply and keep a record of the conversation.
  • Orders. Purchases run through Shopify (hardware, at shop.zknot.io) and Stripe (HashStamp). They collect the name, address, contact details, and payment information needed to take payment and ship an order. We never see or store your full card number — the payment processor handles that.
  • Fulfilment. We use your shipping details to get the parcel to you, and we share them with the carrier for that purpose.

4. What we publish — and why it is different

Verification records are public by design. Anyone with a code or link can read them, without an account. That is the point of a public rail: a stranger has to be able to check.

Do not put confidential or personal information into a field that will be published. If you enter a public label, treat it as public and lasting.

The committed record — fingerprints, timestamps, chain linkage — is cryptographically committed and we do not edit or delete it. A public label is separate display metadata, and we may suppress or redact one for legal compliance, safety, abuse prevention, privacy, IP complaints, or court orders. Suppressing a label does not alter the committed record.

Buying hardware does not publish your identity. A device or seal record identifies the article, not the purchaser. We do not publish who bought what.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data, ever.
  • We do not run behavioural advertising or share data with ad networks.
  • We do not use your files or records to train models.

6. Who processes data for us

Cloudflare (hosting, CDN, security) · Shopify (hardware orders) · Stripe (payments) · our email provider · shipping carriers. Each receives only what it needs to do its job.

7. How long we keep it

Order and tax records are kept as long as the law requires — generally seven years. Correspondence is kept while it is useful and then deleted. Published records are permanent by design, as described in section 4.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Contact ops@zknot.io and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

One honest limit: we cannot delete a committed verification record, because the chain is append-only and removing an entry would break verification for everyone else relying on it. If a published label contains your personal data, we can suppress the label — tell us and we will.

9. Children

Our products are not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Questions: ops@zknot.io · ZKNOT, INC., PO Box 993, Salt Lake City, UT 84110, USA.
