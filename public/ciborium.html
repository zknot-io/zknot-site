Ciborium

Hardware wallet · prototype · founding run open

A hardware wallet whose build you can check. Every Ciborium will be made under one roof, by us, and every step of making it will leave a signed record on verifyknot.io that you can read before you trust the device with a key.

What's different

Hardware wallets already check that the device is genuine. Ledger, Trezor and others run a check that the chip inside is theirs. By their own account, that check can't tell you what happened to the device between the factory and your desk.

Ciborium is built so you can check that part too. Each unit will carry a code, and that code will resolve to its build history, one signed record per step:

  • the bare board arriving, and the batch it came from
  • the secure element being provisioned, and the public key it produced
  • the firmware written to it, by hash, so you can compare it with the published build
  • its test results
  • it being sealed, packed and shipped

Nothing is handed to a contract manufacturer. Board, element, firmware, test, seal and shipping all happen in one place, so there is one party to hold to account, and that party publishes its records.

We looked for a wallet that publishes its build like this and didn't find one. If you know of one, tell us.

What it won't claim. A signed record shows that our key signed a statement about a step. It doesn't make the statement true, and the time on it is our clock, not an outside authority's. Each step's record will say what it does and doesn't show, the same way every other ZKNOT article does. Ciborium is in design: no unit exists yet and there is no ship date. This page describes what we intend to build, not something we've built.
