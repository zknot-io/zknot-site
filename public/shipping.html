Shipping Policy

Last updated 2026-08-05

We are a small manufacturer in Salt Lake City. Most articles are made in small batches or to order, so we would rather tell you honestly how long that takes than quote a dispatch time we cannot hold.

1. Where we ship from

All orders dispatch from Salt Lake City, Utah, USA.

2. Where we ship to

We currently ship within the United States, including APO/FPO/DPO addresses.

International orders: we do not offer international checkout yet. If you are outside the US and want an order, email ops@zknot.io and we will quote it individually. We would rather arrange it properly than have a parcel stuck in customs.

3. How long dispatch takes

ItemTypical dispatch
In-stock itemsWithin 5 business days
Made-to-order itemsWithin 15 business days
Corporate or event runsQuoted individually

These are dispatch times, not delivery times — carrier transit is on top. In practice most in-stock orders leave well inside 5 days; we quote the outer bound rather than the best case, because a shipping date is a promise and we would rather beat one than miss one.

If we cannot dispatch within the time above, we will email you before we go any further, tell you the new date, and give you the choice of waiting or cancelling for a full refund. You never have to chase us for that notice.

4. Carriers, cost, and tracking

We ship by USPS or a comparable carrier, chosen for the size and value of the order. Shipping cost is calculated at checkout. Every order ships with tracking, and we email the tracking number when the parcel leaves us.

5. If something goes wrong

  • Parcel not arrived? Tracking sometimes stalls before it moves. If it has not arrived within 10 business days of the tracking's last update, email us and we will chase the carrier or send a replacement.
  • Arrived damaged? Photograph the packaging and the article before removing anything, then email us. We will replace it. Do not discard the packaging until we have spoken — the carrier may need it.
  • Wrong address? Tell us as soon as possible. Before dispatch we can usually fix it; after dispatch we cannot redirect a parcel, and a re-ship will need to be paid for.

Risk of loss stays with us until the parcel is delivered to you. If it is lost in transit, that is ours to resolve, not yours.

6. A note on tamper-evident goods

Some of what we ship is tamper-evident by design. If a parcel arrives with a seal or wrapper already broken, do not accept it as intact — photograph it and contact us. That is exactly the condition these articles exist to make visible, and we will replace it without argument.
