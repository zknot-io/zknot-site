Thank you — here is exactly what happens next

Your payment went through Stripe, and Stripe has emailed you a receipt. Nothing else is needed from you. The steps below are ours.

WhenWhat
NowStripe's receipt is in your inbox. It carries the order reference — quote it if you write to us.
Within 5 business daysYour article is packed and dispatched from Salt Lake City by USPS with tracking. If we cannot hold that, we email you first with the new date and the option of a full refund.
At dispatchWe email the tracking number.
On arrivalEvery article carries a serial and a short code. Enter it at verifyknot.io to see its record.
